Splashdown Zone

When Freedom 7 came down in 1961, the Navy had a recovery ship parked in the Atlantic waiting for it. Park the ship in the wrong spot and your astronaut bobs around alone. Your job: aim a launcher so the payload splashes down next to the recovery ship.

You’ll use the range equation R = vᵢ² sin(2θ) / g — then find out exactly when it stops telling the truth.

Before you start: calculator in DEGREE mode, g = 9.8 m/s², round to 3 significant figures (angles to 0.1°). Each check unlocks the next one.

Watch out: the inverse sine gives you 2θ, not θ. And remember sin(x) = sin(180° − x).
